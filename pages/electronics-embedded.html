Surveillance Car

A student-built mobile surveillance project combining a remotely controlled car with an adjustable camera for observation and exploration.

Recognition

  • Created for the SAMB Annual Day Exhibition 2023, where it was considered the best among the projects.
  • Invited to showcase at Nepal Army Headquarters for Army Day 2023.
  • Also invited to the 8th Youth Scientist Summit 2024 and featured at the Reliance Educational Network IT Fest.

How it works

The car is controlled from a web page. Four controls operate its wheels, while sliders adjust the camera’s pan and tilt, flashlight intensity, and driving speed.

The movement and camera controls serve separate purposes: the car can be positioned with the wheel controls, then the camera can be turned to inspect the surroundings without moving the vehicle. The speed and flashlight sliders provide additional control during the demonstration.
